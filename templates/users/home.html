Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers to common questions about safety and matching.

How does the matching score work? +
Our system uses a TF-IDF Vectorizer combined with Cosine Similarity algorithms to compare your resume skills with job listings. It computes a final match percentage from 0% to 100% based on skill matching, role alignment, and description overlap.
Is my Gmail connection safe? +
Yes, completely. We connect using Google OAuth 2.0 protocol and request Read-Only access scopes to scan job alerts. We never store password data, and we cannot compose, send, or delete emails.
Can I upload Word (.docx) resumes? +
Yes. The system supports both PDF files (processed via PyMuPDF) and Word documents (processed via python-docx library) to retrieve and map text inputs.
