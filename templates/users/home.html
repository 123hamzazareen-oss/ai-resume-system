Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers to common questions about safety and matching.

How does the matching score work? +
Our system looks at the skills on your resume and compares them to the standard skills needed for your job field. It then gives you a score from 0 to 100 based on how many skills match.
Is my Gmail connection safe? +
Yes, 100% safe. We only use official Google sign-in to read job-related emails. We never save your password, and we cannot send or delete emails.
How does it match jobs to my resume? +
We read the text of your resume and the text of the job emails. We then use a smart comparison method to see how similar they are and show you a percentage score.
